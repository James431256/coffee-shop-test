Our story

Your new local, on Partington Lane

Ruby's opened its doors in summer 2026 with one simple aim: give Swinton a friendly little place to grab a proper coffee, sit down for a bite, and catch up with friends at the end of the week.

By day we're a coffee shop, pouring lattes and flat whites, toasting bagels and crumpets, and making sandwiches and toasties to order. From Friday afternoon the lights go down and Ruby's becomes a cosy bar for the weekend.

  • Coffee shop Tuesday to Friday, breakfast served all day
  • Bar open Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings
  • Oat, almond and soy milk, plus vegan cheese on request
