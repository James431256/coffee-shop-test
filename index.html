Our story

A little shop with a big kettle

Ruby's started with a simple idea: the town deserved somewhere to sit, slow down and drink a really good cup of coffee. What began as a secondhand espresso machine and three wobbly tables is now the place locals pop into on the way to work, on the school run, and on lazy Sunday mornings.

The shop is run by Ruby and a small team who know most regulars by name (and their order by heart). We roast-to-order with a local roaster, bake our cakes in-house every morning, and source our milk, bread and veg from producers just down the road.

  • Locally roasted beans, ground fresh for every cup
  • Cakes baked on site every morning
  • Dogs, laptops, prams and muddy boots all welcome
